## Who we are
- Pinnacle Debt Solutions helps people in South Africa who are overwhelmed by debt.
- We specialise in Voluntary Surrender of Estate (VSE) matters, working with our partner attorney.

## What is Voluntary Surrender of Estate (VSE)?
- A formal legal process under the Insolvency Act where a person who cannot pay their debts applies to the High Court to surrender their estate for the benefit of their creditors.
- It is a court process, not an informal arrangement, and it has legal consequences. Whether it is suitable depends on the person's full financial situation, which a consultant assesses.

## What happens next?
- A consultant phones the person at the time they chose, reviews their debts, income and assets, and explains the options that may apply, including whether VSE is suitable at all.
